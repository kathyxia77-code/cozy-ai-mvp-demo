User Agreement

We value the privacy of you and your baby.

To provide personalized breastfeeding plans, we need to collect and process information about you and your baby, such as your breastfeeding goals, maternity leave duration, your baby’s age, height and weight, breastfeeding records, and other breastfeeding-related information you choose to provide.

Subject to applicable laws, some of this information may be considered health-related information and children’s personal information. We process it only to provide and improve the breastfeeding plan and Voice Log services.

We do not use your personal information to train artificial intelligence models. Where required by law, we rely on your consent. If you are a legal guardian, your consent also applies to your baby’s personal information associated with your account.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting Dataprivacy@momcozy.com. Withdrawal will not affect processing conducted before withdrawal, but it may prevent us from continuing to provide services that require this information.
